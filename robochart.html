Overviewing the State Machine, Verification and Deadlock Freedom components of RoboChart

by Marietta Galea

RoboChart is one of the modelling languages developed by the RoboStar team. It is a domain-specific language for modelling robotic systems defined by their inputs, outputs and operations. In this talk, we will introduce the core principles of RoboChart, explore how robotic controllers can be specified using timed state machines, and provide a high-level overview of how these models can be formally verified to establish important safety properties.
